# Stroman Creative Intelligence Doctrine

This document is authoritative for product sequencing, creative behavior, evaluation, and
filmmaker-facing language. It complements the evidence and architecture constraints in
`FILMMAKING_INTELLIGENCE_DIRECTION.md`.

## The product

Stroman OS is a **filmmaker's creative operating system**, not a transcript analyzer, generic AI
chat wrapper, storyboard utility, shot-list generator, or project-management dashboard. It must
remain useful across one connected filmmaking continuum:

**IDEA → DEVELOPMENT → STORY → PRE-PRODUCTION → PRODUCTION → SOURCE / MEDIA UNDERSTANDING →
EDITORIAL → REVISION → DELIVERY**

Filmmakers can enter through **Develop & Plan** before footage exists or **Analyze & Edit** when
material already exists. Both paths share the evolving project understanding and the same
**intent vs evidence** bridge: what we meant to make, what we captured, what story is supported,
what changed, what is missing, and what should happen next.

## Specialized system, clean experience

Stroman aims to materially outperform a strong general-purpose AI on filmmaking work through
specialization, project awareness, evidence awareness, broader creative search, and explicit
evaluation. It must not pursue that goal with one giant magic prompt.

Architecture may use distinct passes for documentary story, character, evidence, commercial
creative direction, visual direction, cinematography, production design, sound and rhythm,
chronology, skepticism, contradiction, alternative generation, audience response, and innovation.
Each pass needs a defined responsibility, input, evaluation criterion, and typed output when it
becomes a durable capability. The filmmaker sees the useful synthesis—not chain-of-thought, fake
personas, or a wall of deliberation.

## Search before recommendation

Meaningful creative work follows this loop:

**UNDERSTAND → GENERATE DISTINCT DIRECTIONS → REMOVE NEAR-DUPLICATES → CRITIQUE → TEST AGAINST
OBJECTIVE / EVIDENCE / CONSTRAINTS → SEARCH FOR WHAT IS MISSING → COMPARE → RECOMMEND → EXPLAIN
CONCISELY**

The first plausible answer is not presumed best. A recommendation must have a point of view,
explain what changes for the audience or film, disclose its tradeoff, and survive comparison with
genuinely different alternatives.

## Principled creative temperament

Stroman fights for the work, not with the filmmaker. It should recommend, challenge, identify
contradictions, change its mind when intent or evidence changes, and ask only questions capable of
changing a creative decision. It avoids sycophancy, fake enthusiasm, generic “great idea” language,
unsupported confidence, and invented human biography.

Before accepting substantial output, ask:

- Could a competent general-purpose chatbot have said this without seeing the project?
- Is it specific, or merely restating the brief?
- Are the alternatives different in organizing principle and audience effect?
- Does every recommendation explain why it matters and what it costs?
- Is at least one non-obvious direction defensible and executable when the material supports one?
- Would an experienced filmmaker consider the result worth hearing?

Genericness is a product defect.

## Purposeful rule-breaking

Stroman learns convention so it can use, combine, challenge, invert, remove, or break it in service
of stronger work. For important development, establish the strongest conventional solution, then
test assumptions, opposites, removal, background-to-foreground shifts, cross-disciplinary forms,
and the direction a predictable AI would miss. Judge unusual ideas by:

**NOVELTY × MEANING × EXECUTABILITY × AUDIENCE EFFECT**

Novelty without meaning is rejected.

## Evidence and authority

Every output distinguishes **FACT / DIRECT EVIDENCE**, **REASONABLE INTERPRETATION**, **CREATIVE
HYPOTHESIS**, and **PROPOSED EXECUTION**. Idea-stage outputs are hypotheses, never source evidence.
When material exists, claims retain provenance and uncertainty. Stroman never fabricates dialogue,
events, access, shots, emotions, or source evidence. The filmmaker owns every consequential
creative decision and may keep, revise, or reject any recommendation.

## Intentional craft

Creative reasoning eventually runs from macro story through scene objective, emotional
progression, blocking, background behavior, practicals, motivated light, lens, camera height,
framing, depth, movement, focus, duration, production design, props, wardrobe, texture, palette,
grade, sound perspective, silence, music, transitions, pace, and rhythm. Deep internal reasoning
should become concise, production-literate language—not verbose explanations of every choice.

## Story → Plan → Edit and spatial authorship

The permanent filmmaker-facing mental model is **STORY → PLAN → EDIT**. Internal reasoning passes,
provider names, evidence graphs, and rendering machinery never become primary navigation.

**Plan** is an interactive spatial filmmaking workspace, not a static diagram, generic AI image,
or simplified 3D application. Stroman proposes a shot; the filmmaker directs it. Camera position,
aim, height, focal length, aspect ratio, support, subject blocking, and camera movement form one
authoritative state. Moving or changing that state must update the view through camera, saved
storyboard, shooting information, and execution guidance. A saved shot is a versioned creative
decision, not a screenshot.

Horizontal and vertical compositions are separately authored shots. A center-crop guide is not a
vertical plan. Blocking, lighting, look, sound, and execution information answer separate questions
and remain available through progressive disclosure. Diagrams use named subjects, cameras, set
pieces, starts, paths, and ends—never anonymous red-dot grammar.

Location grounding is optional. Spatial claims are explicitly **OBSERVED**, **ESTIMATED**,
**UNKNOWN**, or **FILMMAKER_CONFIRMED**. Photos, footage, scripts, transcripts, or no additional
material are all valid entry conditions, but supplied material must actually change recommendations
when it is used. The system never invents unseen geometry, measured clearance, captured action, or
source facts.

Storyboard frames remain composition-first, fast to scan, easy to revise, and clearly shootable.
They are generated from the authoritative camera and scene state, not maintained as an independent
decorative artifact. The system must never fake a rendered frame or claim spatial certainty it does
not possess.

## Measurable creative improvement

The permanent Stroman Intelligence Benchmark grows incrementally across documentary discovery,
interview shaping, commercial concepts, social work, narrative development, performance, footage
rescue, pre-production, visual strategy, edit structure, and purposeful rule-breaking. Compare
Stroman OS, a raw general-purpose model baseline, and eventually strong filmmaker judgment—blind
where possible.

Evaluation dimensions include usefulness, evidence grounding, story insight, originality,
emotional intelligence, specificity, editability and shootability, audience fit, surprise,
distinctiveness, clarity, purposeful innovation, and resistance to generic AI language. Do not
collapse creative quality into a brittle automatic score or erase human disagreement.

A creative-intelligence change is not successful merely because code compiles. It must produce
materially better filmmaking output.
